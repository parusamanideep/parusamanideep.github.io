01 Who I am

        

          

            
I'm a ground segment systems engineer at Dhruva Space, with 4+ years building the link between satellites and the people who fly them.

            
My work spans the whole chain: requirements and architecture, SDR-based ground stations across VHF, UHF, S- and X-band, CCSDS-compliant TT&C, DVB-S2 high-data-rate downlinks, RF link budgets, antennas, and the integration and testing that make a ground station ready for operations.

            
I've supported the Thybolt-1 and Thybolt-2 missions, and I'm currently designing high-throughput, multi-satellite downlink systems for LEO. I'm also a licensed ham radio operator.
