I'm an Electronics & Communication engineer from B V Raju Institute of Technology, drawn to the point where circuits meet the airwaves.

            
My internships took me from building 5G wireless systems at IIT Hyderabad to designing real-world electronics at Retron Energies. I enjoy taking an idea all the way through — sketch, schematic, board, firmware, and the bench where it finally comes alive.
